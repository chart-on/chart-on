# Sponsor charton

charton is free and open source under Apache-2.0. Sponsorships pay for the time that goes into it: fixes, new features, docs and answering issues. The biggest sponsors are listed on the [landing page](/).

## Get listed

Sponsors are listed by the total they have sent, biggest first. To be listed, email [contact@charton.dev](mailto:contact@charton.dev) after your payment with:

- **The transaction hash** and the network you used.
- **The name** to show, and **the link** it should open.
- **A logo**, if you want one instead of the name: SVG, or PNG at least 320 px wide, on a transparent background.

You can also sponsor without being listed: say so in your email, or just send the payment.

Listing is not an endorsement.
